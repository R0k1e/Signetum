# Agent-team delivery

Give each independent change a registered member branch and worktree. A task
brief refers to its approved record, requirement IDs, owned paths and existing
validation commands. It selects work without narrowing the approved outcome.
Preserve the delivery worktree and unrelated state.

Members use targeted checks and `ready`. Only named ready tips enter a batch;
final validates its exact frozen source. Significant findings and accepted
clause edits belong in source journals through `just protocol`, not a second
handwritten report. `current`, `history` and `status-all` provide the working
set, original rationale and standing answers on demand.

Reconcile findings before final. The generated delivery review binds approved
outcomes, standing answers, affected historical evidence and admitted member
receipts. Later unadmitted work does not enter that review. Show the generated
review before explicit delivery; passing validation does not authorize it.
Publication confirms the exact remote identity before moving the local target.
Existing legacy changes keep their original ledger and accepted documents until
explicitly migrated.
