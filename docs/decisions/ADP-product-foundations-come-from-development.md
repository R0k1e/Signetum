# Product foundations come from development

Status: Accepted
Review date: 2026-10-29
Supersedes: None
Superseded by: None

## Context

During the approved workflow alignment, the owner established three foundations:
the repository and Skill serve development, the product develops itself through
its own protocol, and Perdura supplies real problems and lessons to absorb.
Convenient installation, distribution and use are product requirements. Future
positioning centers on reliable development with large teams of coding agents.

## Decision

Optimize development outcomes and review cost, including the cost of maintaining
the protocol. Use the shipped protocol and implementation owners for Signetum's
own development. Record self-use problems and repair generic mechanisms instead
of keeping a private self-development bypass.

Treat Perdura as an originating application and continuing source of verified
experience. Trace each proposed migration to an actual problem, existing solution
and acceptance evidence. Generalize reusable mechanisms; leave application paths,
selection algorithms and repository policy with their owners. Do not claim that
experience transfer means downstream adoption has already completed.

Distribute one standard Skill with repository-owned instructions and commands.
Installation and first use are acceptance boundaries, including the actual
launcher and its literal arguments. Extend existing tools rather than introduce
an account service, cross-machine platform or automatic workflow adoption.

Position the product for reliable development with large agent teams. Explain
concrete failures, repairs and observed outcomes. Distinguish a demonstrated
mechanism from measured deployment scale or general reductions in defects and
review time. Publish cases when their evidence supports them, without invented
numbers or unsupported reliability guarantees.

## Approval and enforcement

The owner's explicit direction authorizes these product principles. Individually
identified current clauses retain their rationale through the source journal.
README, product guidance, ordinary self-development and existing installed-Skill
acceptance apply them. This change authorizes documentation and local validation,
not site publication, outreach, delivery or a downstream Perdura modification.
