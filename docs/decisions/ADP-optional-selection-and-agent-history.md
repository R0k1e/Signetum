# Optional selection and agent-organized historical working sets

## Context

The owner explicitly removed dedicated historical working-set adaptation and
specified that structured selection is optional, with full validation by default.
The prior boundary added document-format responsibilities and retained implicit
impact narrowing when no provider was configured.

## Decision

Delete working-set source declarations, the Markdown constraint parser and
row-preserving migration extensions. Agents read and organize historical sources
into ordinary reviewable proposals, preserving reasons and source references.
Approval and accepted-history mutations retain their existing owners. Generic
legacy decision inventory and explicit source-span previews remain unchanged.

Without an explicit selection provider, changed and ready execute the complete
declared member-safe gate and required checks. Impact rules cannot narrow this
execution or authorize skipped tests through inheritance. Admission requires
complete corresponding evidence. Final retains its full declared gate, including
final-only checks. World-dependent checks keep their existing final boundary.

An explicit provider may select checks and targets through the existing shared
boundary. Preserve mandatory checks, conservative unexplained-path fallback,
source-bound evidence and the single supervised execution owner. Do not migrate
repository-specific selection algorithms into Signetum.

## Approval and enforcement

The owner's two explicit instructions authorize this replacement of the affected
adoption clauses. The consolidated change record binds scope, acceptance and
execution autonomy. Engine, generated and installed tests verify behavior; stop
at readiness without modifying Perdura or delivering.
